Security & Regulatory Compliance Specifications

Password Security: Hashed with bcrypt (salt rounds: 10). Plaintext passwords never stored.
Session Security: Cryptographically signed JWT tokens with 7-day expiration.
Role-Based Access Control: Backend enforced permissions on every mutating endpoint.
Audit Trail: Immutable log records user identity, action description, and UTC timestamps.
Edit-Check Integrity: Re-validates clinical rules before permitting query resolution.
Interoperability: Proof-of-concept exports for HL7 FHIR R4 JSON & CDISC ODM-XML v1.3.2.

Architecture aligns with New Drugs and Clinical Trials (NDCT) Rules 2019, GCP guidelines for clinical trials in India, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.
